1. Curated & Custom 6-Bay Arrangement

Every SpaceVanta modular tray houses six calibrated warm-paper compartments inside a brushed slate-and-gunmetal exterior. Start from the curated six-piece arrangement or swap individual compartments inline while honoring tray variety rules.

2. Direct Scene Pools & Cross-Pool Allowances

Scene pages provide focused single-pool adjustments for that desk setup. Full cross-pool curation is centralized on the dedicated Bundles page with automatic state handoff so your selected bays are never reset.

3. Space Express & Made-to-Order Dispatch

Ready-to-ship items consolidate cleanly. When a tray includes a Made-to-Order piece, your shopping bag lets you choose between combined or split dispatch before proceeding.
